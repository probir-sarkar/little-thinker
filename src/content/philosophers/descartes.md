Descartes decided to throw out everything he believed and start again, keeping only what he could prove. To make it fair, he even pretended that a sneaky demon was putting fake pictures into his head.

Everything crumbled. How do you know your memories are real? How do you know the room is there? Then he noticed something: whatever the demon does, someone is being tricked. Someone is <em>thinking</em>.

So: <strong>I think, therefore I am.</strong> That was his one unshakeable stone. He then tried to build the rest of knowledge on top of it - a fair bit of it collapsed, and that was useful too.
