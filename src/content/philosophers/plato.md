Plato was Socrates' student, and he wrote the questions down. He built a school called the Academy, where people argued about ideas for 900 years.

His most famous picture is the <strong>cave</strong>. Imagine people chained in a cave facing a wall. Behind them is a fire, and puppets walk in front of the fire. All the prisoners ever see are shadows, and they think shadows are everything there is.

One prisoner gets free. He walks out and is blinded by the sun. Slowly he sees trees that are <em>more real</em> than the shadows. Plato's question for you: how do you know you are not still in the cave?
