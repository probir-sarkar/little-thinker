Fair does not always mean <em>the same</em>. If two children get the same slice of cake but one has just eaten, being 'equal' can be quite unfair.

Philosophers split this into two ideas: <strong>same for everyone</strong> (equality) and <strong>what each person needs</strong> (equity). Most real arguments about fairness are really an argument about which one you are using.

Try it on something small: there are three biscuits and four people. Now what?
